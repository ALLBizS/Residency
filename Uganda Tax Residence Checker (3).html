    Residence rules are from the Income Tax Act (section 7). PAYE uses the standard monthly bands (nil to 235,000; 10% to 335,000; 20% to 410,000; 30% above, plus 10% over 10 million) and a flat 30% for non-residents, before NSSF or other adjustments. Secondary income is taxed at the flat rate entered (40% by default), whether resident or not. It is a guide only. Please confirm with URA or a tax adviser, especially for borderline cases.
